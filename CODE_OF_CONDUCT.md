# Code of Conduct

This is a small project and the aim is simple: a friendly, useful place to learn and build.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, give specific and kind feedback, assume good intent, and welcome people at every experience level.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, or anything that makes others feel unsafe.

**If something goes wrong:** the maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

**Reporting:** to report a conduct problem privately, use the repository's private reporting form (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") and say it is a conduct report, or contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile.
